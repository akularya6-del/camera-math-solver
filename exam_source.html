General Instructions

  • This test contains 50 questions. Each question carries 5 marks for the correct answer.
  • For each incorrect response, 1 mark will be deducted (negative marking).
  • No marks are awarded or deducted for unattempted questions.
  • The clock is set at the server and the countdown timer at the top right corner of your screen will display the time remaining for you to complete the exam. When the clock reaches zero, the examination will end by itself.
  • Questions in this paper cover: Relations & Functions, Inverse Trigonometry, Matrices, Determinants, Continuity & Differentiability, Applications of Derivatives, Integrals, Differential Equations, Vectors, 3D Geometry, Linear Programming, and Probability.
  • Use the Question Palette on the right side to navigate between questions.
  • Use the Save & Next button to save your response and go to the next question.
  • Use Mark for Review & Next to flag a question and return to it later.
  • To change a previously saved answer, click the option and press Save & Next again.
  • Once you click Submit, you will not be able to return to the exam.
